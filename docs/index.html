Effortless check‑in.

Three shortcuts that check your kids in and out of Brightwheel by themselves, while you are still parking.

Requirements

  • An iPhone running iOS 27 Settings → General → About → Software Version
  • A Brightwheel login The email and password you use in the app
  • Your 4-digit check-in code The one you tap on the school's iPad

Setup

  1. Download all three files

    Get the shortcuts

    Tap each file ending in .shortcut, then tap Download. Skip the .zip.

  2. Open them in the Files app

    Files, then Downloads, then tap each one. Nothing looks like it happened when you downloaded them; this is where they went.

  3. Answer three questions

    Your email, your password, your 4-digit code. One at a time.

    Finish with Skip Setup, not Add Shortcut.

    Add Shortcut does nothing at all on iOS 27. Skip Setup keeps everything you typed.

    Check the first letter of your password.

    The box capitalizes it, so hunter2 is saved as Hunter2 and signing in fails later.

  4. Run it once at school

    Open Brightwheel Check In and run it by hand. It signs you in and scans the code by the door — neither can happen on its own.

  5. Add the arrival triggers

    Each shortcut shows you how the first time you run it. Use Arrive for both, on morning and afternoon times that do not overlap.

    Set Location Services for Shortcuts to Always, or nothing ever fires.

What it knows about you

Your password stays on your phone
Stored inside the shortcut, sent only to Brightwheel to sign in.
The download holds nothing about your family
Everyone downloads the same file. It asks Brightwheel who your children are every time it runs, so a new sibling or a room change needs nothing from you.
Apple signed the file
Which is why your iPhone opens it without warning you about where it came from.
It stops rather than guessing
If it cannot tell who your children are, or one turns up in two rooms, it sends nothing and says so. A half-finished check-in is worse than none, because you would believe it worked.
